03 / DRUG-DELIVERY RESEARCH

Research Assistant · Apr–Nov 2025
Academia Sinica

Carrier preparation and formulation optimization

At Academia Sinica, I prepared polymeric nanoparticles and liposomes and compared preparation and processing conditions. I used particle-size and surface-charge measurements, HPLC-based drug-loading analysis, and release studies to evaluate the resulting carriers.

Comparing batches made formulation choices concrete: a change in preparation can affect dispersion, loading, and the measurements used to judge a carrier. This work strengthened my interest in connecting material properties with delivery performance.

Questions I want to investigate

DOCTORAL RESEARCH INTERESTS

For doctoral study, I am interested in how biomaterial and carrier design can improve therapeutic function—from tissue repair to targeted drug and gene delivery. Industry experience showed me how much development depends on experimental evidence; I now want deeper research training to design and test the systems that generate that evidence.

01 / MATERIAL–TISSUE INTERACTIONS

How does design shape function at the treatment site?

My work with scaffolds and carrier formulations motivates this question. I want to study how material structure, local retention, and cell interactions influence tissue repair or productive delivery—not only how much material reaches a site.

02 / DELIVERY & CELLULAR FATE

What happens between uptake and expression?

Developing nitric-oxide delivery systems made the location and duration of activity important to me. For genetic medicines, I want to understand how trafficking, cargo stability, and access to the relevant cellular compartment affect functional delivery.

03 / FUNCTIONAL EVIDENCE

What evidence should guide development?

My preclinical and regulatory experience motivates my interest in what a measurement can support. I want to connect material properties to meaningful outcomes—such as tissue function or therapeutic expression—with controls that distinguish design effects from formulation or model differences.
