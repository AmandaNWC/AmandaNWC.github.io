ABOUT THESE NOTES

I keep the observation
and the question together.

I am Nai-Wen (Amanda) Chi, a Regulatory Scientist at Coastar Therapeutics in San Diego. I hold an M.S. in Biomedical Engineering from National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan. My background spans biomaterials, cartilage-repair devices, preclinical research, and regulatory affairs. My earlier research includes nitric-oxide delivery for osteoporosis and injectable hydrogels for cartilage repair.

I am interested in targeted nucleic-acid delivery and in vivo immune-cell engineering, particularly the intracellular steps that connect particle uptake to functional expression. These notes collect my reading and proposed follow-up questions. Each note separates published findings from my interpretation.
