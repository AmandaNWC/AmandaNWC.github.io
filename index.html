Questions I want to investigate

DOCTORAL RESEARCH INTERESTS

My current focus is targeted nucleic-acid delivery and biomaterial-cell interactions, especially delivery systems for immune-cell engineering.

01 / CELL TARGETING

Which interactions lead to productive uptake?

I am interested in how receptor binding and internalization influence delivery, and when surface abundance is an inadequate guide to target selection.

02 / INTRACELLULAR FATE

What happens between uptake and expression?

I want to understand how trafficking, cargo stability, and access to the relevant cellular compartment affect functional delivery.

03 / FUNCTIONAL EVIDENCE

What should a delivery experiment measure?

I am interested in experiments that distinguish particle accumulation from functional expression, using controls that account for formulation differences.
