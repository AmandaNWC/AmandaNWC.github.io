Questions I want to investigate

DOCTORAL RESEARCH INTERESTS

In a PhD, I want to design and test materials for tissue repair or drug and gene delivery. My industry experience showed me how important good experiments are to developing a treatment. I want to build the research skills to do that work.

01 / MATERIAL–TISSUE INTERACTIONS

How do materials work at the treatment site?

I want to understand how a material’s structure affects how long it stays at the treatment site and how it interacts with cells. I would test whether these changes improve tissue repair or delivery into the cells that need treatment.

02 / DELIVERY & CELLULAR FATE

What happens between uptake and expression?

My nitric oxide research made me think about where a treatment acts and how long it lasts. For genetic medicines, I want to learn what happens after cells take up a carrier. Does the cargo remain intact, reach the right place, and lead to expression?

03 / FUNCTIONAL EVIDENCE

What results would support further development?

I want to test whether a change in material design improves tissue function or expression of a therapeutic protein. I would use controls to check whether the improvement comes from that change or from other differences in the formulation or model.
